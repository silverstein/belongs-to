# Ellery Family Revocable Trust

Fictional example for belongs-to.

## Page 1

Agreement dated April 12, 2019. Sam Ellery and Dana Ellery are spouses. They file federal income tax jointly. Sam and Dana are the grantors and initial co-trustees of this revocable trust.

## Page 6

Article 6. At the first death, the trust continues. The surviving spouse serves as sole trustee.

## Page 8

Signed by Sam Ellery and Dana Ellery on April 12, 2019. A notary acknowledged both signatures.
